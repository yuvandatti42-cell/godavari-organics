1. Grounds for Returns & Free Replacements

We accept returns, replacements, and refunds under the following conditions:

  • Transit Damage or Breakage: The bottle, container, or vacuum seal arrived broken, cracked, or punctured during transit.
  • Spillage or Leakage: Oil, ghee, or grain spillage occurred inside the shipping packaging.
  • Incorrect Item Received: The product variant, size, or item received differs from what was ordered on armafarms.com.
  • Quality / Freshness Defect: Any natural quality irregularity or seal compromise detected upon receipt.

2. Exchanges Policy

We do not accept exchanges. In order to maintain strict organic food safety and hygiene standards, items cannot be swapped or exchanged. If any product is delivered damaged, spilled, or defective, a direct full refund is provided.

3. Non-Returnable Conditions

Because our products are edible organic grocery items, consumer health and food safety are our highest priorities. In accordance with Indian Food Safety (FSSAI) guidelines:

  • Products that have been substantially consumed or used cannot be returned.
  • Returns requested past the 7-day delivery window are not accepted.
  • Items damaged due to improper customer storage (e.g. leaving cold-pressed oils under direct tropical sun or moisture) are not eligible for return.

3. Return Shipping & Restocking Fees

We believe in 100% transparent and customer-first service:

  • Return Shipping: FREE (₹0). Our integrated logistics partners (Shiprocket, Delhivery, Bluedart) will arrange complimentary pickup from your address.
  • Restocking Fee: FREE (₹0). We never charge any handling, restocking, or administrative fees.

4. Step-by-Step Return / Replacement Process

  1. Notify Us: Reach out to us within 7 days of delivery via WhatsApp at +91 90100 88063 or email at godhavariorganicfoods@gmail.com.
  2. Provide Order Details: Mention your Order ID (e.g. GOF-123456) and share a quick photo or video of the defect/damage.
  3. Resolution in 24 Hours: Our customer care team will review and approve either a Free Express Replacement or a Full Refund within 24 hours.
  4. Courier Pickup: If return of the damaged unit is required, our courier partner will pick it up at no cost to you.

5. Refund Timelines

  • Prepaid Orders (Razorpay / UPI / Cards): Refunds are initiated immediately upon approval and reflected in your bank account or payment app within 3 to 5 business days.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders: Refunds are credited directly via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) or IMPS bank transfer within 24 to 48 hours of verification.

6. Order Cancellations

You may cancel an order anytime before it has been dispatched from our Eluru fulfillment hub by contacting us via WhatsApp (+91 90100 88063). Once an order has been handed over to the express courier, it cannot be cancelled in flight, but is fully covered under this 7-day replacement policy upon delivery.
