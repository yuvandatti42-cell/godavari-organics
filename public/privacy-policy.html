Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

At Godavari Organic Foods (Arma Farms, reachable at armafarms.com), we respect your personal privacy and are committed to safeguarding your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how it is used, and the security measures we take to protect your data in compliance with Indian Information Technology regulations.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit, browse, or place an order on armafarms.com, we may collect the following information:

  • Contact Information: Your name, email address, phone number, and delivery shipping address.
  • Order & Transaction Details: Products purchased, quantities, pricing, order dates, and payment status.
  • Technical & Analytics Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and browsing interaction via Google Analytics to enhance user experience.

2. Payment Security & Banking Details

We do NOT store or have access to your credit card, debit card, UPI PIN, or net banking passwords.

All online financial transactions are processed securely through Razorpay, India's leading RBI-authorized PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateway, utilizing 256-bit bank-grade encryption.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively for legitimate store operations:

  • To process, fulfill, and ship your farm harvest orders via our logistics partners (Shiprocket, Delhivery, Bluedart).
  • To send you automated order confirmation invoices and package tracking updates via email and WhatsApp.
  • To provide responsive customer service and process returns or refunds.
  • To improve store performance and ensure website security.

4. No Third-Party Selling or Renting

We will never sell, rent, trade, or distribute your personal contact information to any third-party marketing agencies or external brokers. Your information is shared only with verified operational partners (payment gateway and courier services) strictly necessary to deliver your package.

5. Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain your shopping cart contents and remember your session. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings at anytime.

6. Data Retention & Customer Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections, or request deletion of your account by contacting our data protection desk.
