ARMA ORGANIC — MILLET IMAGE ASSETS

This folder contains 10 separate square PNG images: one product image and one benefits infographic for each millet.

Naming convention:
01–02: Organic Foxtail Millet (Korralu) — product / benefits
03–04: Organic Kodo Millet (Arikelu) — product / benefits
05–06: Organic Little Millet (Samalu) — product / benefits
07–08: Organic Barnyard Millet (Oodalu) — product / benefits
09–10: Organic Browntop Millet (Andukorralu) — product / benefits

For an app or website agent, use files ending in _Product.png for product cards and files ending in _Benefits.png for the product detail / benefits section.
